Whitened Teeth Tutorial

Learn how to whiten teeth in Photoshop using the Hue/Saturation adjustment layer.

Steps Done: 0/8

Chapters Complete: 0/1

Chapter 1: Hue/Saturation and Masking

Incomplete
1

Open your image in Photoshop.

2

Create a new Hue/Saturation adjustment layer.

3

In the Properties panel, select "Yellows" from the dropdown menu.

4

Reduce the Saturation to -49 to remove yellow tones from the teeth.

5

Adjust the Lightness slider to +66 to brighten the teeth.

6

Invert the layer mask on Hue/Saturation using CTRL + I on Windows or CMD + I on macOS.

7

Equip the Brush Tool with white foreground color with 100% opacity and 0% hardness.

8

Gently brush over the teeth to reveal the whitening effect.
